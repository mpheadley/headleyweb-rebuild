## Part 7 — Video Strategy

### Platform Strategy

**Facebook — Primary (start here)**

Phase 1 leads come from NE Alabama Facebook groups. Facebook is where the HVAC owner, the plumber, the contractor is scrolling at 8pm.

- **Reels (60-90 sec)** — reaction videos, walk-in stories, "I Googled [trade]" reveals. Get pushed to non-followers. Best reach.
- **Native video posts (2-5 min)** — portfolio walkthroughs, site critiques. Post natively, not cross-posted from Instagram.

**Groups remain the highest-leverage channel.** A Reel that goes in three local business groups reaches more decision-makers than a month of organic page growth.

**Do NOT:** share YouTube links on Facebook. Upload natively every time.

**YouTube — Secondary (start Phase 2)**

- **Long form (5-10 min)** — StoryBrand best websites breakdowns, full portfolio walkthroughs, "I Googled [trade]" with full commentary
- **YouTube Shorts (60 sec)** — auto-generate from the long-form edit

**LinkedIn** — Text posts often outperform video here. Walk-in stories as text posts. 60-90 sec talking head for personal brand moments.

### Three Recording Formats — Pick One, No Decisions Needed

| Format | Setup | Best topics |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction/discovery** | Phone selfie or phone pointed at screen | "I Asked ChatGPT," "I Googled [trade]," reaction to a bad website |
| **Critique/breakdown** | Desktop screen share, talk over it | StoryBrand best websites, portfolio walkthroughs |
| **Walk-in story** | Talking head, 60-90 sec, phone | What happened in the field this week — honest, no editing |

### Video Content Pipeline — Ordered Sequence

**Tier 1: Record this week (zero prep required)**

**V1. "I Asked ChatGPT to Recommend a Plumber in Anniston"**
Already drafted in `content/blog/drafts/`. Matt action items: open ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google. Type the query. Record screen + reaction. 60-90 seconds. Highest-share video in the pipeline.

**V2. ESC walkthrough**
Site is live. 90-second screen walk. "I built a professional website for emotional support chickens — here's what makes it actually work." No editing needed.

**Tier 2: After first walk-in day**

**V3. Walk-in story (talking head)** — Record same day. What you saw, what surprised you.

**V4. "I Googled [plumber] in Anniston — here's what happened"** — Screen recording, 90 seconds. Show the map pack. Becomes landing page at `/audit/plumber-anniston`.

**Tier 3: After Between Worlds ships**

**V5. Between Worlds case study** — "From referral-only to a full digital sales system." The video that closes leads in discovery calls.

**V6. Heather Florals** — "I built my wife's florist website." Hold until Between Worlds is live — two portfolio pieces is a portfolio, one is just your wife's site.

**Tier 4: After Southern Legends hits 5+ profiles**

**V7. Southern Legends** — "I built a local storytelling magazine to prove NE Alabama small businesses deserve better."

**Standing quarterly: StoryBrand best websites — trade critique series**
One per trade, quarterly. Screen share, 5-7 minutes, two real trade websites side by side. Plain language. Record first, write the blog post from the transcript.

Order: HVAC → Plumbing → Contractors → Landscaping → Restaurants

### Repurposing Workflow — One Recording, Four Outputs

1. **Record** — phone selfie or screen share, 5-10 min for long form
2. **Cut short version** — 60-90 sec highlight for Reels
3. **Captions** — upload to Captions.ai or Descript, export with captions (non-negotiable — most people watch muted)
4. **Transcribe** — paste transcript to Claude: blog post draft + LinkedIn text post + email version
5. **Distribute:**
   - Facebook: upload Reel natively + post in relevant groups with custom hook
   - LinkedIn: upload short cut OR post text adaptation
   - YouTube: upload long form (when YouTube phase starts)
   - Email list: adapted version via Resend

### Weekly Rhythm (Phase 1)

One 60-90 second video per week:
- Mon: pick topic from pipeline (work through Tier 1 → Tier 2 in order)
- Tue/Wed: record (no editing — just trim start/end)
- Thu: add captions, paste transcript to Claude for blog + LinkedIn drafts
- Fri: post natively to Facebook, add to relevant groups with custom intro

CRM task ID 429 — recurring weekly.

## Part 9 — Distribution Checklist (every post)

> A great post nobody sees is worthless. Spend as much time distributing a post as writing it.

For **every** published post:

- [ ] **Facebook groups** — Calhoun County groups, NE Alabama business groups. Write a 2-3 sentence hook first. Don't just drop a link.
- [ ] **Personal Facebook** — your network is your first audience
- [ ] **Email nurture list** — every new post goes to list via Resend
- [ ] **Google Business Profile post** — short update with link, helps your own local ranking
- [ ] **LinkedIn** — repurpose as shorter text post with link

**Post-specific extras:**
- [ ] **Reel/TikTok** — for posts with video component
- [ ] **Tag featured businesses** — for hyperlocal posts and roundups
- [ ] **Cross-post to Southern Legends** — for hyperlocal content that overlaps

### Publishing Rules

- Alternate SEO track (keyword gap) and Social track (shareable). Don't do 4 SEO posts in a row.
- All posts get OG/Twitter meta, proper slugs, and internal links to services page
- Each post ends with CTA: "Get Your Free Video Audit" or "Schedule a 15-Minute Call"
- Roundup posts (6-10) can be written now — they don't require client work
- AEO posts (11-18) are important for positioning but your clients don't search these terms yet. Lower urgency.
