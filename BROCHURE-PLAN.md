## Version 2 — Trifold (next build)

### Why trifold

The one-pager has pricing, steps, trust copy, and the problem all visible at once. Cognitive load is high. A trifold sequences the read — each panel has one job. Feels intentional as a physical leave-behind. People keep trifolds.

### Format

Standard letter-size (8.5×11) C-fold trifold.
- 6 panels total: 3 outside, 3 inside
- Panel widths: outer panels ~3.875in, inner fold panel ~3.625in (folds short)
- Print double-sided, fold center-right panel inward

---

### Panel Layout

```
OUTSIDE (folded — what sits on the counter)
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│   Panel 6    │   Panel 5    │   Panel 4    │
│  Back cover  │  Back panel  │ FRONT COVER  │
│  (faces      │  (hidden     │ (faces up,   │
│   away when  │   inside)    │  seen first) │
│   closed)    │              │              │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘

INSIDE (when fully opened — read left to right)
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│   Panel 1    │   Panel 2    │   Panel 3    │
│    GUIDE     │    PLAN      │   PRICING    │
│  + empathy   │  (3 steps)   │   + CTA      │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

### Panel Content — Spec

**Panel 4 — Front Cover (seen first, folded)**
- Job: Stop. Intrigue. One action.
- Content: Tagline in Fraunces italic ("Your website should show up like you do.") + brand name + QR code to headleyweb.com/audit
- NO pricing, NO steps, NO problem copy
- Dark background (--color-dark) with white tagline + terracotta accent
- Small "headleyweb.com" in subdued type at bottom

**Panel 5 — Inside Flap (first panel seen when opening)**
- Job: Name the problem. Make them feel it.
- Content: "Most service businesses in Northeast Alabama are losing leads they can't even see." + 3 pain points (not 6 — pick the sharpest three). No subheads.
- Light background, terracotta left border

**Panel 6 — Back Cover (faces away when closed, seen last)**
- Job: Who is this guy? Contact info.
- Content: Headshot (see photo notes below) + "Matt Headley, Jacksonville AL" + phone + email + website + "Get found. Get calls. Get booked."
- This is what the prospect sees when they flip it over

**Panel 1 — Inside Left (Guide / Empathy)**
- Job: Trust. Why Matt.
- Content: 2-sentence empathy statement ("As a husband, dad, and former pastor living right here in Jacksonville...") + 5 benefit bullets (clean checkmarks, not paragraphs)
- Optional: small laptop photo (`laptop-matt-headley.webp`) — makes it human

**Panel 2 — Inside Center (The Plan)**
- Job: Make it feel simple. Remove fear.
- Content: 3 steps with terracotta numbered circles. Each step: title + 1-sentence description. This is the widest panel — give the steps room.
- Sub-line at bottom: "Flat rate. You own everything. No contracts."

**Panel 3 — Inside Right (Pricing + CTA)**
- Job: What does it cost? What do I do?
- Content: 3 tier names + prices only (no feature lists):
  - Get Found — $495
  - Get Calls — $1,495 ← Best Value
  - Get Booked — $1,995
- One line: "Monthly care plans from $49/mo — ask about availability."
- CTA button: "Scan for your free site checkup" + QR repeated
- Contact line: (256) 644-7334
