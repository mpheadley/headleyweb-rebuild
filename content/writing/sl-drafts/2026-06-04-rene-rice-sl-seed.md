### On what made her build the chapel

> "At 40, I was told I would never have a baby. And at 43, there was a different plan in mind, and my husband — and so at 44, I had a baby, as we were finishing up that wedding chapel. And my husband turned 52. So that was what I was going to do on the weekends on a sideline job."

> "By that time, my stepchildren that were 22 and 25 were graduated from college and getting married, and then I turned pregnant. Okay? So it's a totally different deal. That's funny, isn't it?"

---

### On why the chapel went quiet

> "And so I've not been pursuant of that. So it has not been — it is on the market as we speak."

> "I have devoted my whole last 19 years to Cheyenne Renee Rice. My little bambino that I was told I would never have. It completely changed my life."

> "She's 19. She just completed her first year at Alabama. Just incredible kid. I cannot tell you how blessed I am."

---

### On what she wants for it now

> "I would love for her to come down there and rent that for me."

> "There's no place in Calhoun County that can touch it for the size wedding it is, for the price point it is. And it should be successful and it is not because I have devoted my whole last 19 years to Cheyenne Renee Rice."

> "We are in a position that we can do that at this point in time in our life. So we would love for someone to take what we started and make something of it."

---

### On the chapel itself

> "The problem is nobody wants to leave, because it's an intimate setting."

> "In five minutes, I can transform the chapel — the coat closets have tables and tables. They leave the chapel to go to the reception. In five minutes, the flip happens."

> "We were renting the wedding chapel for $1,500. You can't find a venue cheaper than $1,500. And we don't care what anybody wants to make on top of that."

---

### On her mother and the land

> "My mother has the house to the right — the Roebuck House, built early 1900s, two-columned, porches, old outbuildings. When we first couldn't get the wedding chapel, we bought that old house to redo as a wedding venue. Then mother decided she wanted to live there. She's 87, still lives there."

> "My mother said there is a call on that property. And we just need to figure out what it is."

---

### On the whole arc

> "My sister and I are Christians — our whole — this whole thing is a whole God thing. If you had time for my life story today, you would hear it all."

## The Story Shape (Iris's read — not a draft)

Rene built a chapel at 44 — the same year she had the baby she was told she'd never have. She was finishing the building when the pregnancy began. She has run a travel agency since 1979. She has not had time to run the chapel because the baby took everything. The baby is now 19 and at Alabama. And now Rene is 63, or thereabouts, with a paid-off chapel that she built with her hands and her faith, and her mother's 87-year-old voice echoing across the property: *There is a call on that place.*

That is the essay. Not the wedding chapel. The woman who built it while becoming a mother she wasn't supposed to be.

**The SL angle:** At 40, she built a thing for the life she thought she was living. At 44, she became someone new. The chapel sat for 19 years, waiting. Her mother is 87. The child is in college. And now someone is finally asking what the place was built for.

## Gap Audit (Before Any Draft)

Before any prose is written, these gaps need filling:

1. **Time and place anchor:** Where exactly was she when she decided to build the chapel? At 40, what did she think her life would look like? Give me the scene.
2. **The pregnancy discovery:** When did she find out she was pregnant? Was she in the chapel? What happened in the body?
3. **The day it opened:** Did they have their first wedding? What was it like?
4. **The day she stopped:** The moment she realized — not a decision, a drift — that the chapel was being left behind for Cheyenne. When did that happen?
5. **Her mother's line:** "There is a call on that property." When did her mother say that? What were they doing? This is a scene. It needs a time and a place.
6. **Cheyenne:** What does Cheyenne know about the chapel? Has she ever been married there? Does she want to?
