# Whitman & Berry — Seed
*April 16, 2026*

---

## The connection

Kindred spirits. Both rooted in the particular, both convinced the local and the bodily are where meaning lives.

- **Whitman:** expansive — the self as cosmos. "I contain multitudes."
- **Berry:** specific — the self as place. "It may be that when we no longer know what to do, we have come to our real work."

Same argument. Different angles.

---

## Matt's version

The farm was Berry. The mania was Whitman. The rebuilding is where they meet.

Matt used Berry for preaching and farm work — not a borrowed reference, a native language. Whitman showed up in the mania — the expansion, the feeling of containing everything, the self as too large to hold.

The collapse was both of them failing at once. The rebuilding is learning to hold both.
