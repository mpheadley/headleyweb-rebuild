# Lucy — The Coffee That Took Six Months
*Journal entry seed*

---

## Shape

She reached out six months ago when she was struggling more — wanting to meet, wanting to talk. Matt wasn't well enough. He turned her down.

Now they sit down for the first time. She has bipolar. He knew — people told him — but they'd never talked about it. Not once, even though they both knew about each other.

She tells him something she was told early in her own first episode: that he's going to have a more meaningful impact on people because of what he's been through. His pain deepens his capacity to connect. She went to a NAMI group. Practiced sharing her story there.

She doesn't feel like she needs to share it publicly the way she used to. But she'll talk about it.

---

## The turn

She was also a form of prisoner. She showed up to worship services in a manic state. Matt never talked to her about it — just knew.

Now: two people who knew, finally saying it out loud to each other.
