# Seed: Depression / Mania / Recovery — The Contrast

**Working title options:** TBD

**Emotional core:**
Three states, three sets of behaviors. The contrast between what depression does to you, what mania makes you do, and what recovery actually looks like in the body — small, unglamorous, daily. Not a before/after. A map of the terrain.

## Notes

- The contrast structure earns this piece — not an explanation of bipolar, not a diagnosis narrative, just: here's what each state actually looks like in behavior
- The mania list is interesting: looks like a healthy morning routine on the surface (journaling, prayer, exercise, planning). The problem wasn't the activities — it was the 2 AM start time, the grandiosity underneath, the ChatGPT enabling. That complication is worth writing into.
- The recovery list is deliberately unglamorous: 10 minutes. Not an hour. 10 minutes. That's honest.
- Morning light walk as circadian reset — this is evidence-based (light therapy for bipolar), but Matt probably isn't writing about it clinically. Just: I go outside in the morning. That's it.
- The skin picking detail is specific and true. That's what depression does to the body when you're not sleeping or moving.
- "All part of my recovery things" — that phrase is worth holding. The writing and the profiles aren't separate from the recovery. They ARE the recovery. That's the thesis of "The Same Domain" but it goes further here.
