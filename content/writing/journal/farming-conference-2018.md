# Seed: The Farming Conference — Weaver, 2018

**Status:** Seed / scene fragment
**Potential placement:** Act One / Act Two of memoir — before the farm, the dreaming toward it
**Cross-reference:** CLT book (faith + land + redemption), Plainspoken Blueprint (wound chapter)

---

## The scene

2018. Matt was still pastoring at Weaver. Before the farm. He attended Noah Sanders's farming conference — *Redeeming the Dirt*. This was not yet his life. It was something he was walking toward.

*What did the room feel like? What did he leave wanting?*

---

## What this seed holds

- The farm came after that conference. Then it was gone.
- But the land is still there. And now he's building something else — The Aisle, the expo, the ministry in a new form.
- The arc: walked into a room full of farmers not yet being one → became a farmer → lost the farm → is now building something that carries the same values in a different form.
- The conference was about *redeeming the dirt* — reclaiming land, faith, and vocation. Matt's whole story is a version of that title.

## Related voices — farming / homesteading community

**Justin Rhodes**
- Homesteader, filmmaker, YouTuber (Permaculture Chickens — millions of subscribers)
- Around Matt's age. Large family. Public health journey. Storyteller.
- Speaks at permaculture and homesteading gatherings.
- Matt: "he's a big inspiration to me... lots in common. I'd love to meet him."
- Reachable: community-oriented, approachable. SL profile candidate or book peer.
- Angle: reach out at a permaculture or homesteading event, or via social. Not a cold ask — a peer connection.

**Erin Benzakein / Floret Flower Farm**
- THE name in specialty cut flower growing. "Floret Farm's Cut Flower Garden," "A Year in Flowers."
- Skagit Valley, WA. Huge following of flower farmers and growers.
- Heather Florals + Bloom Bar + flower farming is the natural bridge.
- Not a direct book connection, but an Aisle / Heather Florals reach — collaboration or feature.

**Joel Salatin / Polyface Farm**
- Regenerative farming, local food systems, entrepreneurship.
- "Lunatic Farmer." Speaks widely. Connects to market/community ecosystem.
- Quotes available. Permaculture/homesteading community crossover with Justin Rhodes.

**Wendell Berry**
- Already a load-bearing voice in the PB book: "The impeded stream is the one that sings."
- For the CLT book and memoir: displacement, belonging, care of the land, what we owe the land we've been given.
- Age 89+. Direct collaboration not realistic. Epigraph permission is standard.
- His essay "It All Turns on Affection" is the text most relevant to this material.
