**On Matt's response:** Said "psychiatric center at UAB for depression" — not bipolar, not mania. Either the bipolar diagnosis hadn't happened yet by November 20, or he was being careful about how much to share. The resignation was Ash Wednesday (March 2025), the hospitalization was later. Timeline needs checking.

**Context in Matt's response:**

- Heather working at the museum
- Matt applying for jobs

**What Matt remembered vs. what the texts show:** Matt recalled telling Tammy he'd been diagnosed bipolar and was manic when he resigned. These screenshots don't show that exchange — either it happened in a later text not captured here, or memory has compressed events. Worth checking if there are more texts.
