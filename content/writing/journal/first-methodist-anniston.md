## Relationship to ministry-grief.md

These are not the same piece. They share source material but serve different functions:

- **ministry-grief.md** — the 20-year career grief, told from the end. Loss as subject. The arc is backward: from the resignation toward the career that was lost.
- **first-methodist-anniston.md** — the Anniston First years as lived forward. Arrival, daily life on Gurnee, the Tammy relationship, Jacksonville First, the resignation morning. The arc is forward: from arrival toward departure.

They crosslink. The grief piece references Anniston First repeatedly ("Anniston First, where I used to be associate pastor" — from No Shade). This piece is where you earn that line. They are separate pieces that need each other.

**Do not combine.** The ministry-grief piece is about what was lost; this piece is about where it happened. Combining them risks either making the grief too institutional or the Anniston First story too heavy with retrospective weight.

## Pre-arrival context — what he brought with him (June 2020 journals)

From voice memo April 28, 2026 — Matt reading from his physical journal, ~16 months before the Anniston First offer arrived:

**June 24, 2020:** *"I've been missing the practice of gratitude and optimism. Kim recommended a book to me about optimism, probably because she recognized my lack of it lately. My ego fixates on problems... I'm beginning to feel more hopeful and attached to my work with the church."*

Matt's note reading it back: *"This is surprising — I can see I was already struggling with my mental health, optimism, back in 2020. And other people were recognizing it. Kim Westbrooks especially. I was also feeling detached from my work. And this was approximately six to eight months before I resigned [from Weaver]. I think the job [at Anniston First] was offered to me in November, roughly October or November of 2021."*

**June 28, 2020:** *"I want to live in the present, not the past. I'm afraid of letting go — like my life will be meaningless without preserving the good things I've done, or I won't be able to escape my past wrongdoings."*

**Editorial significance:** He didn't arrive at Anniston First healthy and then get ground down. He arrived already struggling — detached, ego-fixating on problems, afraid of meaninglessness, with Kim Westbrooks already noticing. The bait-and-switch landed on someone who was already wounded from Weaver. This changes the shape of the Anniston First story.

## Gap audit — in progress (April 28 2026)
*Matt's words verbatim*

### Scene 1a: First walk into the building (pre-hire)
*"probably during my college years singing in the chamber choir and jsu accappella choir. vasst space, acoustics, stained glass window, sitting in the chancel area. likely around 2007"*

### Scene 1b: First walk in as incoming staff
*"came in on easter sunday 2021, day before i was to begin working as staff. filled with people, joyous choir, jason with praise band. sitting in pews near the front. excited about the prestige of taking on music director role, and to be recognized as incoming person. people had been saying 'how did we get him?' because i had been a pastor at weaver for 6.5 years. so also carrying some of those hard feelings, but they were subtle and overpowered by excitement with the change and new role at a bigger more prestigious church"*

Not officially recognized that day — *"no i was not, just in the pews with my family. not officially recognized"*

### Scene 2: The bait-and-switch hire
*"i was in the room with her when it landed. blindsided by it. in her office."*

Tammy: *"detached and professional"*

Room detail: *"she was across from me"* — Tammy across the desk, Matt facing her. Not behind the desk, not beside him. Across.

### Scene 3: Shannon Jenkins hallway
Already written in the published Shannon Jenkins profile (opening paragraph). Cross-reference only. Verbatim from published piece: *"I knew who he was but didn't feel I should take up space. That was 2023, in a cinder block hallway at Anniston First: pastors' offices on one side, mine on the other. Shannon Jenkins was meeting with Kyle and Tammy. I was the music director. Shannon stopped. Shook my hand. Asked my name."*

### Scene 4: The title and raise fight — two conversations
**First conversation (within 6 months of hiring):**
*"i had tried to get a raise within 6 months of my hiring because i was grieved/bothered by my demotion. tammy pointed me to april, who listened but was a hard no."*

**Second conversation (associate pastor offer):**
*"years later, it was a conversation about me taking the associate pastor role after kyle had left, or shortly before he did. and she told me there would be no change in my pay for the new role and increased responsibilities."*

Both in her office, across her desk.
