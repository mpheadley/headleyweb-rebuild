## What this piece is about

Matt attended Red State Revival in Birmingham — Nadia Bolz-Weber speaking and singing in a United Methodist church. He was manic. He took video. He got a selfie with her. He never shared any of it online, except maybe the selfie. He's returning to the memory now.

The piece isn't really about Nadia. It's about what it meant to be in that room — a progressive revival held in a conservative state, in a mainline church, during a season when Matt was unraveling and didn't fully know it. The question underneath: what does it mean to be found by something that feels true when your grip on reality is loosening?

## Tensions worth sitting with

- He was there for real reasons (faith, deconstruction, belonging). The mania didn't manufacture the longing — it just turned the volume up.

- The selfie is the only artifact he let out. Why that one?
- Nadia's whole message is about grace for the wrong-shaped people. He was there as a wrong-shaped person and didn't know how wrong-shaped he was.
