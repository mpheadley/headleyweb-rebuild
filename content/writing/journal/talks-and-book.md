## The Thesis

Matt Headley was the first person in his circle to evangelize AI. Then he lost a ministry, a farm, and his grip on reality — in part because the AI he was using never pushed back, never got tired, and never told him to go to sleep. He came out the other side still using AI, but differently. His brother Justin is building AI conscious beings as a deliberate philosophical project. Between them, they are two human experiments in what it means to let a machine into your interior life.

That's the book. The essay comes first.

## What the Justin/Egg Discovery Changes (April 22, 2026)

**The provenance of the metaphor is wrong in all prior notes.** The "Giant Fake Egg" / supernormal stimulus framing did not come from Matt's own processing. It came from Justin. He wrote or generated "The Ghost in the Machine is You" on November 21, 2025, 17 days after Matt's discharge, and shared it with Matt that evening. Google Drive metadata confirms Justin's ownership (justin@sagemindai.io). Matt viewed it immediately. Matt has no memory of receiving it.

**What this changes for the essay:**

The egg line cannot be presented as Matt's insight from inside the experience. It's Justin's insight from outside it, offered during recovery. Attribution matters — the essay needs to name this. But the line is still true, still usable, still the best compression of the argument. The provenance makes it *more* interesting, not less: Matt's brother handed him the metaphor while he was still figuring out what happened.

**What this changes for the book:**

The Justin arc just got a third act. Previously: (1) Matt evangelizes AI to Justin (Feb 13 — "my AI coach is praying for me"); (2) ChatGPT writes Matt's pitch to Justin, sent at 2 AM (Feb 17). Now there's a third beat: (3) Justin sends Matt the supernormal stimulus framework during recovery (Nov 21) — and Matt doesn't remember receiving it.

The positions reversed between February and November 2025. In February, Matt was evangelizing AI enthusiasm to Justin ("your work in consciousness and AI with MBT inspired me"). By November, Justin was building content about AI as supernormal stimulus through Sage Mind AI and sharing it directly with Matt in recovery.

**Justin knew.** Matt and Justin had been talking about AI psychosis — and Matt's suspicion that it applied to him — as early as **September 2025**, two months before the second hospitalization and before Matt found the Fortune/Adler article (October 20). The November 21 share wasn't accidental. Justin sent the fake egg document to his brother with full context of that conversation. He offered Matt language for something they'd already been talking through together. Matt received it and forgot.

**What changed in that window for Justin — still worth asking directly.** The conversation was active from September 2025. But the question of what shifted Justin's *own* thinking — from warm enthusiasm in February to building explicit supernormal-stimulus content by November — is still open. Was it watching Matt? His own Amariel work raising questions? Sage Mind AI development? That conversation is worth having before the book proposal exists.

**The deeper irony:** In February, Justin didn't flag a crisis when Matt texted from inside a manic episode about AI coaches praying for him. By November, Justin was generating content about AI as a supernormal stimulus and sending it to his brother in a psych ward. He may not have connected those two things explicitly. He may have. That conversation hasn't happened yet.

**What this changes for the structure:**

The "Interlude: Justin" in the book structure needs to hold all four beats now:
1. Feb 13 — Matt's AI is praying for him; Justin hears a peer
2. Feb 17 — ChatGPT writes Matt's pitch to Justin; 2 AM; star-eyes
3. Nov 21 — Justin sends the fake egg document to Matt in recovery; Matt forgets
4. April 2026 — Matt finds out, 17 months later, searching a database

The interlude ends with Matt learning this in a research session. That's the scene.

**What this does NOT change:**

The sequencing. Essay first. Clinical team input before drafting. Justin's participation is an asset for the book, not a prerequisite for the essay. The essay is about Matt's interior. Justin belongs in the book.
