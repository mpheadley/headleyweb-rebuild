**BEAT 1 — the mania from the inside**

filming myself lip sync dancing while running the bcs and during early morning walks on the ladiga trail. filming selfie videos, carrying around a tripod with my newly purchased iphone 16 pro max iphone mounted on it.

filming myself promoting the farmers market, with an app running an ai written script prompter on my phone, spending hours posting and sharing on social media, adding stickers and avatars

**BEAT 2 — the resignation**

i felt angry, but exhilarated. it was messy.

i was working 16-20 hour days, sleeping three or four hours a night. everything felt holy. wild success was inevitable.

the farm was holy. everything was holy.

i left the pulpit and stepped into the flower field. last month, i became a full-time farmer. it's been terrifying and tender. sacred and sweaty. and honestly? it's saving me.

**BEAT 3 — tim stigers**

ive thought often of a pastor mentor "tim stigers" a presbyterian minister turned hospice chaplain, who went through a sort of depression after he was fired because his preaching wasn't good enough, and reflected aloud with me and the bible study he was leading with me and our young adult group in ministry "i lost my calling"

i still feel i haven't completely lost my calling, though i thought i had.

**BEAT 4 — driving home from dance**

i was driving when i listened. it made me ache and angry/jealous. driving sage back from dance on thursday evening in april. telling heather about how it made me cringe.

felt jealous of jerod and shannon.

[Jerod, verbatim from podcast:] "I know that's something you feel like you've been called to do and you're walking in your calling and what you're supposed to do with your life."

[Shannon, verbatim from podcast:] "I just always really felt a calling, felt a calling to help people. And I really thought for a long time that that calling would be on a church staff somewhere. **BEAT 5 — the first crack**
