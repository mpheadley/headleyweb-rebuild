## Matt's Ventures — Magazine Integration Options

matthewheadley.com/card = personal card hub with 6 tiles: The Aisle, Plainspoken Blueprint, Gather, Bloom Bar, Southern Legends, MarketDay. Plus role as Anniston Farmers Market Director.

**Option A — "From the Publisher" spread (recommended):**
A single 1-page spread near the back. Clean. Matt's photo, 3-sentence bio, 4–6 QR codes (one per major venture + farmers market). This is standard practice — publishers advertise in their own publications. Signals confidence, not crass. Could double as a "meet the ecosystem" page for readers who find the magazine at the expo and don't know the full picture.

**Option B — Individual small ads:**
One quarter-page ad per venture, scattered through the magazine as page breaks. More commercial. Works if you want to model the ad format for other businesses.

**Option C — Classifieds/directory page:**
Minimal. A single column: name, one line, QR code. Like a Yellow Pages entry. Very SL-appropriate editorial register.

**Matt's own ad in addition to selling ads:** YES. Do it. Run a full-page self-promotional ad on p2 or inside back cover. Standard practice in literary publishing. It also normalizes the magazine as a commercial entity. Revenue model: SL + The Aisle both in the ad, QR to theaisle.app/expo, QR to southernlegends.us.

**QR codes with coupons:** Viable for vendors who want a trackable offer (e.g., "$10 off your first Gather studio subscription," "Free expo ticket upgrade with code SLVOL1"). Easy to implement — unique URL per coupon code, track in Airtable. Could be offered as an ad upgrade tier.
