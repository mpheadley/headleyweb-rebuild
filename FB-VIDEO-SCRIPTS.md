## AI Video Generation — Decision Log

**Decision: No AI-generated talking head video.**

Adam Harper (the main local competitor) already does low-production selfie video. You can't out-authentic him on format — he's already there. The differentiation is substance: local credibility, no funnel, Southern Legends, GETUP/Main Street Anniston relationships. None of that shows up in AI-generated footage.

The specific risk: Jacksonville and Anniston business owners are skeptical of anything that feels like it came from a marketing machine. AI-generated video is the exact signal that undermines the "flat rate, no contracts, real person" positioning. If anyone spots it — and people are getting better at spotting it — it kills the trust the content is trying to build.

**Where AI video generation is appropriate:**
- B-roll: screen recordings with animated callouts, text overlays, transitions
- Thumbnail graphics
- Captions and subtitle styling
- NOT the talking head — that has to be Matt on his phone

**See also:** `memory/video-gen-animated-sites.md` — Kling 3.0 + frame extraction for design-playground lab experiments. That's a separate use case (animated site demos), not client-facing marketing.
