## Weekly 60-Second Intro

BNI gives each member 60 seconds. This should feel like a person talking, not a pitch deck. One problem, one solution, one ask.

---

I build websites for local service businesses in Northeast Alabama.

Most of the businesses I work with have a site — but it's not doing anything for them. Either they built it themselves, or someone built it and disappeared, and now it just sits there while their competitors show up first on Google.

Here's what I fix: when someone in Jacksonville or Anniston searches for what you do, I make sure you show up. On Google Maps. In AI search. On their phone in under three seconds.

I also run Southern Legends — an editorial site covering Northeast Alabama stories. Built it the same way I build client sites. Nearly 10 minutes average time on the page. That's what the right approach looks like.

Flat rate. No contracts. You own the site from day one.

If you know a plumber, HVAC tech, contractor, or any local service business who's frustrated that their website isn't generating leads — I'd love an introduction.

I'm Matt Headley, Headley Web & SEO. Get found, get calls, get booked.

## AI Search Live Demo (use when you have 90 seconds extra or during feature slot)

Pull up your phone. Open ChatGPT. Type: "local stories from Northeast Alabama" or "small businesses in Calhoun County Alabama."

If Southern Legends comes up — that's the proof. Real, unrehearsed, in the room.

If it doesn't come up yet — that's the point:

> "This is what I'm building toward for my clients. The businesses that get set up for this now show up in these answers. The ones that don't, don't. We're early — that's the window."

Never describe the AI search angle. Show it. The live demo lands harder than any explanation.

## Feature Presentation (10 minutes — when you get a spotlight slot)

> Flesh out after 5+ walk-ins when you have real client stories to add.

**Structure:**
1. Open with the Google Maps demo — pull up Google live in the room, show top 3 results (2 min)
2. Show the AI search layer — open ChatGPT, type a local service query, show Southern Legends if it surfaces (2 min)
3. The three things in their control — checklist format (3 min)
4. Portfolio proof — Southern Legends: "Built this the same way I build client sites. Nearly 10 minutes average time on the page." Show it on your phone. (2 min)
5. Close — free site checkup offer for any BNI member or referral (1 min)

> Same structure as GETUP talk outline. See `GETUP-TALK-OUTLINE.md`.

## Referral Ask (specific = better referrals)

BNI works on specificity. The more specific your ask, the better the referral.

**Weak:** "Anyone who needs a website."

**Strong options — rotate weekly:**
- "A plumber or HVAC tech in Calhoun or Etowah County who knows their website isn't doing anything for them."
- "A contractor who just Googled themselves and didn't like what they saw."
- "A local service business that's been burned by an agency — paid a lot, got nothing."
- "Someone who just opened a new business and needs to show up on Google from day one."
- "A nonprofit in Jacksonville or Anniston that needs a site but doesn't have a budget for it — I'm doing a few free builds right now in exchange for a case study."

## Notes

- Never say "map pack" or "GBP" — say "Google Maps" and "your Google listing"
- Never say "SEO" without immediately explaining what it means in plain language
- The close is always the referral ask, not a self-promo
- Bring brochures — hand one to the secretary and leave a stack if they allow it
- headleyweb.com/audit is the free offer to mention if asked for next steps
- **Portfolio stat to use:** "Nearly 10 minutes average time on the page" (Southern Legends, 90-day GA4 data)
- **Free builds framing:** "I'm intentionally keeping my client load small right now — building free for a couple of local nonprofits in exchange for real before/after data." Honest, strategic, not desperate.
