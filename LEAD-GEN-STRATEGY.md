# Headley Web & SEO — Lead Gen Content Strategy

## Context
Solo web design and local SEO business serving small businesses in Northeast Alabama / Calhoun County. Brand: Headley Web & SEO. Lead gen is the current priority — no paid ads, all organic/community-based.

## Strategy Overview
Build visibility and trust as the knowledgeable local marketing guy before pitching anything. Content and community presence drives inbound conversations.

---

## Active Lead Gen Ideas

### 1. Facebook Group
- Create a group for Calhoun County / Northeast Alabama small business owners
- Show up consistently with useful marketing tips, resources, Q&As
- Long-term play — expect 2–3 months before traction

### 2. Short-Form Video (Selfie Style)
- Post to personal profile and/or Headley Web page
- Format: one practical marketing tip per video, 60–90 seconds
- Tone: local, approachable, real — not polished or corporate

### 3. Free Local Business Audit Offer
- Post in local groups: "I'll review your Google Business profile and website, give you 3 things to fix — free, no pitch"
- High-conversion conversation starter
- Southern Legends credibility supports the offer

### 4. Piggyback on Existing Groups
- Show up in existing local Facebook groups as a helpful voice
- Lower lift than running a group; faster initial visibility

### 5. Small Business Training — May (Upcoming)
- Registered for a small business marketing/digital marketing training session (GETUP, starts May 14)
- Content plan:
  - **Before:** teaser post — "heading to a training, will share what I learn"
  - **During/after:** short videos — "one thing I learned that most local businesses aren't doing"
  - **Recap:** "3 takeaways that apply to any business in Calhoun County"
- Trust signal: shows investment in craft, not just self-promotion
- Note: summarize and apply training content; don't lift proprietary curriculum verbatim

## Farmers Market Vendor Communications

If the Farmers Market Manager job is secured, create a vendor communication group as a city-owned resource — propose it to Karla, not as a personal project.

**Platform recommendation:** WhatsApp group (primary) + SMS fallback for holdouts who won't install it.

**Why WhatsApp over SMS:** Named group, pinned announcements, muted notifications, media organization, message history for new joiners. SMS group texts get messy fast — no structure, reply-all chaos.

**Why WhatsApp over Telegram:** Vendors are farmers, artisans, side hustlers — not tech-forward. Telegram requires a separate app download and account. WhatsApp is already on their phones.

**Why WhatsApp over Facebook group:** Facebook group works for community/visibility; WhatsApp is better for operations (weather cancellations, booth assignments, schedule reminders). Could run both — WhatsApp for logistics, Facebook for community.

**Ownership rule:** Don't create it under your personal name and hand it over. Propose it to Karla as a city tool. Same conflict as doing digital work on city time — clean lane from day one. Raise it as a question: *"How do you currently communicate with vendors — is there a group text or anything?"*
