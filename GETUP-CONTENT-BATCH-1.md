## Draft 3 — "The 60-second website test"

**LinkedIn (primary)**

Three things to check on your business website in the next 60 seconds:

1. Pull it up on your phone. Does it load before you can count to three?
2. Look at the top of the page. Within the first sentence, does it say what you do, who you do it for, and where?
3. Scroll once. Is there a phone number you can tap, or a button that does something — without you having to hunt?

Most local-business sites fail at least two of these.

The fix on each isn't expensive. It's about an hour each, mostly. But the cost of leaving them broken is real — every visitor who bounces is a job you didn't get because your site wasn't ready for the question they were trying to ask.

I'll be walking through fixes, free tools, and live examples at GETUP in August. If you'd rather not wait, my DMs are open and the first 30 minutes is on me.
